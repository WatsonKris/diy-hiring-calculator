When DIY Works, and When It Stops

Start with your networkReferrals and your own network are the right start for early hires. Most founders begin here, and many good hires come from it.

Know when to bring in a trusted talent expertDIY works until you stop getting the quality and speed your team needs. When the months pile up or the role is critical, the right outside recruiter gets you quality and speed the first time.
